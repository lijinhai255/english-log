# Give Information

## Core Vocab & Phrases（核心词汇与短语）

### Give Information（提供信息）

Give information（提供信息）, tell（告知）, inform（通知）；替换词：Provide information（提供信息）, let sb know（告知）, notify（通知）

### Information（信息）

Information（信息）, detail（细节）, message（消息）；替换词：Info（信息，口语化）, news（消息）

### Action(动作)

Tell（告诉）, show（展示）, say（说明）；替换词：Inform（告知）, show（出示）, explain（解释）

### Expression Phrases(表达短语)

Let me tell you（让我告诉你）, The information is（信息是）, I will inform you（我会通知你）；替换词：I’ll tell you（我会告诉你）, The info is（信息是）, I’ll let you know（我会让你知道）

## Practical Dialogues（实用对话）

Dialogue 1: Giving Work Information to a Colleague（给同事提供工作信息）
A (Colleague): Can you tell me the meeting time?
B (You): Sure. The meeting starts at 2 o’clock this afternoon.
A: Where is the meeting?
B: It’s in the team meeting room. I’ll show you the way later.

Dialogue 2: Giving Task Information to the Team Lead（给组长提供任务信息）
A (Team Lead): Can you give me the task progress information?
B (You): OK. I have finished 80% of the task.
A: Good. Tell me when you finish it.
B: Sure. I’ll let you know as soon as possible.

### Information & Notice（信息与通知）

information（信息）, details（详情）, update（更新）, notice（通知）
替换词：info（信息）, latest news（最新消息）, announcement（公告）

### Work-related Info（工作相关信息）

task（任务）, project（项目）, deadline（截止日期）, meeting（会议）
替换词：assignment（工作任务）, plan（计划）, due date（到期日）

### Giving & Sharing（告知与分享）

let you know（告知）, tell（告诉）, share（分享）, inform（通知）
替换词：brief（简要说明）, fill you in（跟你说清楚）, notify（正式通知）

### Common Expressions（常用表达）

I’d like to share some information with you.
Let me tell you the details about…
I’m informing you that…

## Dialogue 1: Giving Information About a New Task

You: Hi Team Lead, could I have a minute? I need to give you some information about the task I’m working on.

Team Lead: Sure, go ahead. What’s the update?

You: I’ve finished the first part of the report. I’m calling to let you know the progress and also share some details about the data （dei ta） I collected.

Team Lead: Great. Can you tell me the main points? Do we need to change anything or adjust the deadline?

You: The data is complete and correct, so no changes are needed. The full report will be ready by tomorrow afternoon. I also want to inform you that I need about one more hour to finish the final check.

Team Lead: That’s totally fine. Just make sure you send it to me by the end of tomorrow.

You: Will do. I’ll send it to you as soon as it’s done. If there’s any new information, I’ll let you know immediately.

Team Lead: Thanks for keeping me updated. Good work.
