# Job Responsibilities

## Core Vocab & Phrases（核心词汇与短语）

### Key Verbs（核心动词）

Do（做）, write（编写）, fix（修复）, test（测试）, work on（从事）；替换词：Create（创建）,check（检查）

### Work Content（工作内容）

Code（代码）, programs（程序）, bugs（漏洞）, tests（测试）；替换词：errors（错误）

### Responsibility Expressions（职责表达）

I’m in charge of（我负责）, I need to（我需要）, My job is to（我的工作是）；替换词：I take care of（我负责）, I have to（我必须）

### Collaboration（协作）

Work with（和……合作）, team（团队）；替换词：Cooperate with（与……协作）, group（小组）

### Practical Dialogues（实用对话）

Dialogue 1: Talking About Job Responsibilities with a New Colleague(与新同事谈论工作职责)
A (New Colleague): What do you do here?
B (You): I’m a developer. My job is to write code and fix bugs.
A: Do you work with a team?
B: Yes. I also test programs with my teammates.
A: That’s clear. Thanks.

Dialogue 2: Introducing Job Responsibilities in an Interview(面试中介绍工作职责)
Interviewer: What were your job responsibilities in your last job?
You: I was an engineer. I was in charge of writing code and testing programs.
Interviewer: Did you fix bugs too?
You: Yes. I often fixed bugs to make programs run well.

### Key Sentence Patterns（重点句型）

My job is to [Work Content].
（我的工作是[工作内容]。）
I’m in charge of [Responsibility].
（我负责[工作职责]。）
I work on [Work Content] every day.
（我每天从事[工作内容]。）
I also [Action] with my team.
（我还和团队一起[动作]。）
I fix [Problem] to make programs run well.
（我修复[问题]以确保程序正常运行。）
