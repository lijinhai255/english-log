# Having a Meeting

## Having a Meeting（开会）

Have a meeting（开会）, attend a meeting（参加会议）, hold a meeting（举行会议）；替换词：Go to a meeting（去开会）, join a meeting（参加会议）, run a meeting（主持会议）

## Meeting-related（会议相关）

Meeting（会议）, agenda（议程）, discuss（讨论）, host（主持）；替换词：Meeting（会议）, plan（议程）, talk over（讨论）, lead（主持）

## Action（动作）

Attend（参加）, discuss（讨论）, speak（发言）, listen（倾听）；替换词：Join（参加）, talk over（讨论）, talk（发言）, hear（倾听）

## Expression Phrases(表达短语)

We are having a meeting（我们正在开会）, I need to attend a meeting（我需要参加会议）, Let’s discuss...（我们来讨论……）；替换词：We are in a meeting（我们正在开会）, I have to go to a meeting（我得去开会）, Let’s talk about...（我们来讨论……）

## Practical Dialogues（实用对话）

Dialogue 1: Attending a Meeting with Colleagues（和同事一起开会）
A (Colleague): Hi, we are having a meeting in 10 minutes. Can you join us?
B (You): Sure. What are we going to discuss in the meeting?
A: We will discuss the new task arrangement.
B: OK. I’ll be there on time.

Dialogue 2: Being in a Meeting（正在开会）

A (Host): Let’s start the meeting. Today, we will discuss the project progress.
B (Colleague): I’d like to speak first. Our team has finished half of the project.
A: Good. Thank you. What about your team?（转向你）
B (You): We are on schedule. We will finish it before the deadline.

## Key Sentence Patterns（重点句型）

We are having a meeting at [Time].
（我们将在[时间]开会。）
I need to attend a meeting.
（我需要参加一场会议。）
Let’s discuss [Topic] in the meeting.
（我们在会议中讨论[主题]吧。）
I’d like to speak in the meeting.
（我想在会议中发言。）
We are discussing the [Project/Task] progress.
（我们正在讨论[项目/任务]进度。）

## Core Vocab & Phrases（核心词汇与短语）

### Meeting Types（会议类型）

team meeting（团队会议），project meeting（项目会议），daily meeting（每日会议）
替换词：group meeting（小组会议），tech meeting（技术会议），standup meeting（站会）

### Meeting Actions（会议动作）

attend a meeting（参加会议），join a meeting（加入会议），start a meeting（开始会议）替换词：take part in（参与）

## Meeting Terms（会议常用）

agenda（议程），on track（按计划进行），behind schedule（进度滞后），action item（行动项）
替换词：schedule（日程），on time（准时），delayed（延迟），task item（任务项）

## Expression Phrases(表达短语)

We are having a meeting（我们正在开会）, I need to attend a meeting（我需要参加会议）, Let’s discuss...（我们来讨论……）；替换词：We are in a meeting（我们正在开会）, I have to go to a meeting（我得去开会）, Let’s talk about...（我们来讨论……）

## Dialogue 1: Reminding a Colleague About a Team Meeting

You: Hi Tom, do you know we have a team meeting at 3:00 this afternoon?

Tom: Really? I totally forgot. What’s the meeting about?

You: We will talk about the latest project progress and this week’s task arrangement. We also need to discuss some technical problems we met this week.

Tom: Got it. Should I prepare anything before the meeting?

You: Yes, you’d better write down your work progress and the difficulties you have. The team lead will ask everyone to share.

Tom: Thank you for reminding me. I will get ready on time.

You: You’re welcome. See you in the meeting room.

You: Good afternoon, everyone. So far, my part of the backend module development is on track. I’ve completed around 75% of the main functions, including data processing and interface testing.

Team Lead: Great to hear you’re on schedule. Have you run into any blockers or challenges that we need to solve as a team?

You: Well, I do have one small issue. The connection between my backend service and the frontend page is sometimes unstable. It works fine in most cases, but it fails randomly during high-frequency testing. I’ve checked the code repeatedly but haven’t found the exact cause yet.

Team Lead: I see. That’s a common integration problem. We can assign a senior developer to help you check it after the meeting. Now, what’s your plan for the next few days?

You: I’ll keep finishing the remaining functions today and tomorrow. Meanwhile, I’ll take notes on when the connection fails so we can locate the problem more easily. Once the issue is fixed, I’ll run a full test to make sure everything works stably.

Team Lead: Perfect. That sounds clear and responsible. Thanks for the honest update. Let’s move on to the next person.

### Key Sentence Patterns（重点句型）

We have a [team/project] meeting at [time] this afternoon.
（我们今天下午[时间]有个[团队 / 项目] 会。）
The meeting is about [progress/blockers/tasks].
（会议是关于[进度 / 阻碍 / 任务]的。）
Let’s get started with today’s [meeting].
（我们开始今天的 [会议] 吧。）

My work is [on track/behind schedule/ahead of schedule] so far.
（到目前为止我的工作[进度正常 / 滞后/提前]。）
I have finished [percentage] of my task.
我已经完成了 [百分比] 的任务。
I’m facing a problem with [system/interface/code].
（我在 [系统 / 接口 / 代码] 上遇到了问题。）
I haven’t found the [cause/solution] yet.
（我还没找到[原因 / 解决方案]。）

My plan next is to [finish/test/fix] the task.
（我接下来的计划是[完成 / 测试 / 修复]任务。）
I need some help with [technical issue].
（我在[技术问题]方面需要一些帮助。）
I’ll keep you [updated/posted] on my progress.
（我会随时跟你[同步 / 更新]我的进度。）
