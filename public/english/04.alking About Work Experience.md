# Talking About Work Experience

## Core Vocab & Phrases（核心词汇与短语）

### Work Experience（工作经历）

Work experience（工作经历）, Job experience（工作经验）；
替换词：Work history（工作履历）

### Time Expressions（时间表达）

Worked（工作过）, For...years（持续……年）, Last job（上一份工作）；替换词：Used to work（过去工作）, Over...years（超过……年）, Previous job（之前的工作）

### Job-related（工作相关）

Company（公司）, Engineer（工程师）, Developer（开发者）；
替换词：Firm（公司）, Tech engineer（技术工程师）, Coder（程序员）

### Expression Phrases（表达短语）

I worked at（我曾在……工作）, I have...years of experience（我有……年经验）；替换词：I used to work at（我过去在……工作）, I have worked for...years（我已经工作了……年）

## Practical Dialogues（实用对话）

Dialogue 1: Talking About Work Experience with a New Colleague(与新同事谈论工作经历)
A (New Colleague): Do you have much work experience?
B (You): Yes. I have 7 years of work experience.
A: Where did you work before?
B: I worked at a small tech company. I was a developer there.
A: That’s great! You must be good at your job.

Dialogue 2: Talking About Work Experience in an Interview(面试中谈论工作经历)
Interviewer: Tell me about your work experience.
You: I have 5 years of work experience as an engineer. I worked at two companies before.
Interviewer: What did you do in your last job?
You: I wrote code and fixed bugs. I worked with a small team.

## Key Sentence Patterns（重点句型）

I have [X] years of work experience.
（我有[X]年工作经历。）

I worked at [Company Name] before.
（我以前在[公司名称]工作。）

I was a [Job Title] there.
（我在那里是一名[职位]。）

I have worked as a [Job Title] for [X] years.
（我做[职位]已经[X]年了。）

I [did] [Work Content] in my last job.
（我上一份工作做[工作内容]。）
