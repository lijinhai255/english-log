# Offering Help in the Meeting

## Core Vocab & Phrases（核心词汇与短语）

### Meeting & Help（会议与帮助）

Offer help（提供帮助）, give a hand（帮忙）, assist（协助）；替换词：help out（排忧解难）, support（支援）

### Meeting-related（会议相关）

Meeting（会议）, discuss（讨论）, presentation（演示）, note（笔记）；替换词：Conference（会议）, talk about（讨论）, demo（演示）, record（记录）

### Action（动作）

Help（帮助）, share（分享）, explain（解释）, pass（递）；替换词：send（发送）, clarify（说明）, hand（递）

## Expression Phrases（表达短语）

Can I help you?（我能帮你吗？）, Let me give you a hand（我来帮你一把）, I can share the file（我可以分享文件）；替换词：Do you need any help?（你需要帮忙吗？）, Let me assist you（我来协助你）, I can send the document（我可以发这份文档）

## Practical Dialogues（实用对话）

Dialogue 1: Offering Help to a Colleague in the Meeting（会议中向同事提供帮助）
A (Colleague): Sorry, I can’t find the project data on my computer.
B (You): Do you need any help? I have the data file on my laptop.
A: Yes, please! Can you share it with me right now?
B: Sure. I’ll send it to you in the team chat at once.
A: Thank you so much! You’re a great help.

Dialogue 2: Offering Help to the Meeting Host（会议中向主持人提供帮助）
A (Host): Oh no, the projector doesn’t work. I can’t show the slides.
B (You): Let me give you a hand. I have the slides on my tablet, we can use it.
A: Really? That’s perfect! Can you connect it to the screen?
B: Yes, I can do that quickly. Let’s fix it together.
A: Thank you for your help! It’s very important for the meeting.

## Key Sentence Patterns（重点句型）

Do you need any help in the meeting?
（你在会议中需要帮忙吗？）

Let me [Action] you.
（我来帮你 [做某事]。）

I can [Action] you a hand to get it done shortly.
（我可以[做某事]您尽快完成这件事。）

Please [Action] the report to the manager.
（请把这份报告[做某事]经理。）

Let me give you a hand with the [Problem/Thing].
（我来帮你处理这个 [问题 / 东西]。）

### Dialogue 1: Offering Help in Daily Scrum

Make: Good morning, everyone. I’m stuck on a login bug. I’ve been checking the code for hours, but I still can’t find why the token validation keeps failing. It’s blocking my whole task, and I’m worried I can’t finish it on time today.

You: I’m sorry to hear you’re stuck on the token issue. I’ve worked on similar authentication problems before, so I know how confusing these errors can be. Can I help you troubleshoot this bug after the meeting? I can sit with you, check the code line by line, and test the token logic together until we find the problem.

Make: That would be really helpful! I’ve tried many ways but still can’t fix it.

You: No problem. I can also help you unblock your task by sharing some debugging tips I used before. If we can’t solve it quickly, I can even take over some small parts of your work so you can focus on the key issue without falling behind the deadline.

Make: Thank you so much! That will take a lot of pressure off me.

### Dialogue 2: Offering Help with Task & Requirements

Matt: Hi team. The new requirements have just been updated, and I need to rewrite a lot of test cases. My workload is really heavy right now, and I’m not clear about several key points in the requirements. I’m afraid I can’t get the test cases done before the deadline.

You: I understand you’re under a lot of pressure with the updated requirements. Would you like me to help you clarify the unclear points after the meeting? I was in the requirement discussion, so I can explain every detail clearly to you.

Matt: Yes, please! That’s exactly what I need right now.

You: I can also help you finish part of the test cases to reduce your workload. You can focus on the most important ones, and I’ll take the simpler parts. We can work together so you can meet the deadline without rushing.

Matt: That’s amazing! I really appreciate your help.

You: Happy to help. Let’s go through the requirements first, then split the work. We can check each other’s work to make sure everything is correct.
