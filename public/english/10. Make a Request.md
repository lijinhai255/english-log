# Make a Request

## Core Vocab & Phrases（核心词汇与短语）

### Make a Request（提出请求）

Make a request（提出请求）, ask for help（请求帮助）, request（请求）；
替换词：Ask for a favor（请人帮忙）, ask（请求）

### Request-related（请求相关）

Help（帮助）, favor（帮忙）, please（请）, can（能）；
替换词：Assist（帮助）, help（帮忙）, could（能，更委婉）

### Action（动作）

Help（帮助）, do（做）, give（给）, show（展示）；
替换词：Assist（协助）, finish（完成）, pass（递）, teach（教）

### Expression Phrases（表达短语）

Can you help me?（你能帮我吗？）, Could you do me a favor?（你能帮我个忙吗？）, Please give me（请给我）；替换词：Could you help me?（你能帮我吗？）, Can you do this for me?（你能帮我做这个吗？）

## Practical Dialogues（实用对话）

Dialogue 1: Asking a Colleague for Help（向同事请求帮助）
A (You): Hi, can you help me? I can’t finish this task.
B (Colleague): Sure. What do you need me to do?
A: Could you show me how to fix this bug? Please.
B: No problem. Let’s look at it together.

Dialogue 2: Making a Request to the Team Lead（向组长提出请求）
A (You): Excuse me, could you do me a favor?
B (Team Lead): Sure. What’s wrong?
A: Please give me one more day to finish the task. I need more time.
B: OK. Finish it on time tomorrow.

## Key Sentence Patterns（重点句型）

Can you help me [Action]?
(你能帮我[动作]吗?)

Could you do me a favor?
(你能帮我个忙吗?)

Please [Action] for me.
(请帮我[动作]。)

Can you give me [Something]?
(你能给我[某物]吗?)

Could you show me how to [Action]?
(你能教我怎么[动作]吗?)

## Core Vocab & Phrases（核心词汇与短语）

Make a Request（提出请求）

make a formal request（提出正式请求）, request assistance（请求协助）, ask for a favor（请求帮忙）, seek support（寻求支持）

Request-related（请求相关）

Help（帮助）, favor（帮忙）, please（请）, can（能）；
替换词：Assist（帮助）, help（帮忙）, could（能，更委婉）

Work & Task（工作和任务）

deadline extension（延期截止时间）， task priority（任务优先级）， performance optimization（性能优化）， submit code（提交代码）

Expression Phrases（表达短语）

Can you help me?（你能帮我吗？）, Could you do me a favor?（你能帮我个忙吗？）, Please give me（请给我）；替换词：Could you help me?（你能帮我吗？）, Can you do this for me?（你能帮我做这个吗？）

Dialogue 1: Requesting Help with a Technical Block（寻求技术故障解决帮助）

You: Hi Jake, do you have a minute? I need to make an important request. I’ve been stuck on a critical API integration problem for nearly two hours, and I just can’t identify the root cause.

Jake: Sure, I’m free now. What seems to be the issue exactly?

You: The interface keeps returning a 500 internal server error when I send POST requests. I’ve double-checked the request parameters, headers, and endpoint URL, but it still fails every time. Could you help me locate and fix this API error?

Jake: No problem. Let me look at your code and the error log together. I’ll help you find out which part is wrong.

You: Also, the system doesn’t give clear error messages. Could you help me read and analyze the backend error log correctly?

Jake: Of course. I’ll explain every step and teach you how to debug this kind of problem independently next time.

You: That would be fantastic. I would appreciate your professional help.

Dialogue 2: Requesting a Deadline Extension Due to Database Compatibility Issues（因数据库兼容性问题请求延期截止日期）

You: Excuse me, Mr. Wilson. May I speak with you about an urgent request?

Wilson: Sure, come in and take a seat. What’s going on?

You: I’ve been working full-time on the payment module development, but I encountered unexpected database compatibility issues that seriously delayed my progress. I wonder if you could approve a short deadline extension to ensure the code is stable and secure before submission.

Wilson: I understand unexpected problems often happen in development. How much more time do you really need?

You: I need at least four more hours to finish testing and performance optimization. Could you give me some advice on how to prioritize the remaining tasks to save time?

Wilson: Alright. I’ll approve the extension. Make sure you update me once you finish every key step.

You: Thank you so much for your understanding and support. I will keep you updated on time.
